ScanMate AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 (version 1.15)

ScanMate AI ("the app", Android package com.scanmateai.app, published on Google Play) is a document scanner with optional AI tools that explain documents. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your scans stay on your phone. We do not run servers and you need no account. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (section 6); a one-time purchase removes them.

1. Scans, PDFs and extracted text

Photos you scan or pick from your gallery, the PDFs made from them, and text read from them (OCR) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Text recognition runs on your device, using Google ML Kit and, for languages you add (for example Bangla, Arabic or Hindi), the open-source Tesseract OCR engine. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a file or use an AI tool (below). Deleting a document or uninstalling the app removes these files.

When you add a text recognition language, its language file (about 3–15 MB) is downloaded once from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, the official tesseract-ocr project). GitHub receives the download request as with any download from their site. No document content is sent.

2. Camera and photos

The ScanMate camera uses your phone's camera only while the scan screen is open, to find the document edges and take the pages. The edges are found by a small AI model and image processing that run entirely on your device; camera images and photos are not sent anywhere. If you turn on "Use Google's scanner", Google Play services' document scanner is used instead (it also runs on your device). The "Phone camera" option uses your phone's camera app. Photos are chosen with the Android photo picker and PDFs with the Android file picker, so the app only sees the files you select. "Save to phone" writes only to the location you pick.

3. AI tools

  • Off (default): no AI is used and no document content leaves your phone.
  • On (your own API key): only when you use an AI tool (Explain, Summarize, Translate, Ask, Exact text, To Excel, To Word, Homework help, AI crop, Suggest a name), the text and/or page images of that document are sent directly from your phone to the AI provider you chose (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Groq), using your own API key. We never receive this content. That provider's own privacy policy applies to how it handles it.

4. API keys, signature, PIN and backups

Your AI provider API key is stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore) and is only sent to the provider it belongs to. A signature you draw is saved only on your device. If you turn on App lock, only a salted hash of your PIN is stored, encrypted, on your device. QR codes and barcodes are read on your device (from the camera or a picture you pick); the list of recent codes is kept only on your device. "Add to Contacts" hands the details to your phone's contacts app, where you save them; the app cannot read your contacts. "Hide private numbers" finds numbers with on-device text recognition. "Back up all documents" creates a file that you choose where to save; we never receive any of these.

When you share photos or PDFs to ScanMate AI from another app, the app receives only the files you shared and keeps them on your device. Read aloud uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. Printing uses Android's print service and the printer you choose.

4b. Sync to Google Drive or a folder (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Backup & sync, the app copies the PDF of each document to the place you choose: a folder you pick on your phone, memory card or in a cloud app, or, where the option is offered, your own Google Drive (the app then asks Google for the drive.file permission, which only lets it see and change the files and folders it creates itself). Copies go only there, directly from your phone; we never receive them. You can stop syncing at any time in Settings.

The app keeps a small log of unexpected errors on your device (no documents, no text from them). It is shared only if you choose "Report a problem" and pick where to send it.

5. Reporting an AI answer

If you tap "Report this answer", the app opens your email app with the answer and the reason pre-filled. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use reports only to improve the app.

6. Ads (Google AdMob) and the "Remove ads" purchase

We do not collect any data ourselves: no analytics, no crash reporting, and no servers that receive your data. The free version shows ads (a small banner on the Home and Tools screens and, now and then, a full-screen ad after an export) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, app and device information, and how you interact with ads. Ads never receive your documents, scans, text or files. How Google uses this data is explained at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalized; you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we only receive a confirmation that the purchase exists, which the app keeps on your device. After the purchase no ads are loaded.

7. Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone: delete a document in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove all of it. We hold no copy and no account, so there is nothing on our side to delete. Advertising data is handled by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly show ads to them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
